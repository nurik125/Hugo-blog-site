Today I completed watching the second season of Tsuki ga Michibiku Isekai Douchuu (TMID), an anime that I love for its characters, dynamics, and well delivered topics. Easy flow of the show facilitates the parallels with real word, making it easier to deduce the key takeaways of it. Such as following the fate is misery, one should have his boundaries set and protected, being rational and helpful is what brings true results.
	Important thing that differentiates this anime from others is <span style="color:red">well minded MC</span>. Throughout the series Makoto Misumi's determination to fight the goddess %% (creature that unfairly praises human rase)%% creates great dynamics that lead to non-standard obstacles. Specifically in the series, goddess grants Makoto ability to talk in all languages excluding human (universal). Due to it, ugly face and frightening mana MC experiences great hatred and decides to collaborate with no-human creatures and maintains neutrality between humans, no-humans and demons specifically.
		But MC is not the single force that keeps this fiction unrepeatable. The world itself build different. Firstly, TMID's goddess is introduced as complete antagonist character and has <span style="color:red">despicable character</span>, unlike other isekai animes, where often god serves as absolute goodness and forgotten after MC's reborn. Secondly, political tensions remain unsolved even in the war period between human and demon races compared to others where they are not introduced in the first place. Lastly, during the series we <span style="color:red">testify 2 other hero isekai</span>, but MC (not claimed hero due to his ugly face). Where each one of three follow their own goals and faith.
			Nevertheless, I can't dare claim Tsuki ga Michibiku Isekai Douchuu completely unique fiction. It uses the foundation developed by other isekai and corrects the dull part with uniqueness. Accordingly, things like MC being the strongest, creating own civilization/country, and establishing merchant lines can be discovered in most animes (Tensura, etc.)
				In conclusion, Tsuki ga Michibiku Isekai Douchuu presents us unimaginable and satisfying plot build from unfulfilled expectations of other isekai; though, creating sort of acceptance. 

Topics: 
- Following the fate is misery
- Ugly look and universal hatred could be overcome by work, talent and results.
- Being hero is not your duty
- Tit for tat is universal strat
- Weak will follow the fool, strong will follow the well minded
- One can set free, when it becomes the strongest
- Determination
