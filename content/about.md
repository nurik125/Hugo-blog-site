Initially this project was developed by Youtuber [Network Chuck](https://www.youtube.com/channel/NetworkChuck) in his video [I started a blog.....in 2024 (why you should too)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dnE7c0ELEH8&ab_channel=NetworkChuck). Watching his video inspired me to make my own version to further explore and expand upon the ideas presented.

## Purpose

Hopefully, this project will serve as a vital resource for me to facilitate the blog writing process and free me from my procrastination.
